Privacy policy

JARVIS personal assistant · effective 27 November 2026 · updated 27 November 2026

Who this covers

JARVIS is a private assistant used only by its owner, on their own Windows laptop. There is no JARVIS server, account system or website backend: everything runs on that laptop, except the optional JARVIS Cloud helper described below, which, if the owner sets it up, runs as a script inside the owner's own Google account.

Google data JARVIS can access

Only after the owner signs in with Google and approves it, JARVIS gets read-only access to:

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): to list, search and read the owner's emails when they ask, and to look for deadlines and payment alerts in recent emails (see below).
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): to list the owner's events when they ask, to give a spoken reminder shortly before an event, and to find exams and deadlines among upcoming events.

JARVIS cannot send, delete, modify, label or mark emails as read, and cannot create or change calendar events.

How the data is used

Email and calendar data is used only to answer the owner's own requests (for example "any new emails?", "read the one from the bank", "what's on tomorrow?") and for event reminders. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone other than as described below.

Deadline tracker. When the owner asks ("any deadlines in my email?"), or once each morning if they have turned that on ("check my email for deadlines every morning"; "stop checking" turns it off), JARVIS reads the subject, sender, date and opening lines of up to 25 emails from the last three weeks that mention words such as deadline, due, exam or fee, plus the titles of upcoming calendar events with such words, and sends that text to the AI model to pick out what is due and when. Only the result, a short title, the due date and the sender's name, is kept, in a file on the laptop, to give reminders; the email text itself is not stored.

Expense tracker. Only when the owner asks ("check my email for payments"), JARVIS reads the subject, sender, date and opening lines of up to 40 emails from the last five weeks that look like bank or UPI payment alerts, and sends that text to the AI model to pick out the amount, the payee and the date of each payment. Only those results are kept, in a file on the laptop; the email text itself is not stored.

JARVIS Cloud (optional, while the laptop is off)

If the owner sets it up, a small Google Apps Script runs in the owner's own Google account so their private Telegram bot can still answer them while the laptop is off. It is created and owned by the owner, runs as the owner, and is not operated by anyone else. It has read-only access to the owner's Google Drive (drive.readonly), to find files and photos the owner asks for and send them to the owner's Telegram chat, and to their Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), to answer "what's on today?" and give a morning briefing. It keeps, in the script's own settings in that account: the Telegram bot token, the owner's Telegram chat ID, the owner's Gemini API key, their city (for the weather), the time zone, the laptop's name and when it was last on, and the titles and due dates of their tracked deadlines (sent by the laptop every minute while it is on, so deadline reminders still arrive). Messages the owner sends while the laptop is off, with file names and calendar entries needed to answer them, are sent to Google Gemini; a short recent history is kept for up to six hours in the script's cache. It answers only the owner's chat and ignores everyone else. It cannot change, delete or upload files, or control the laptop. Deleting the script at script.google.com removes it and everything it keeps.

AI services

To understand a request and word its answer, JARVIS sends the request, and the email or calendar text needed to answer it, to the AI model it is using at that moment: Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, or a model that runs on the laptop itself when offline. This happens only when the owner asks about their email or calendar, or for the daily deadline check if the owner has turned it on. These providers handle that text under their own terms; on free plans, a provider may use submitted content to improve its services.

What is stored, and where

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's laptop, encrypted with their Windows login (Windows DPAPI), so it cannot be used on another computer or account.
  • Email and calendar contents are not written to JARVIS's log files or its long-term conversation archive. Like any conversation, the last few exchanges (up to 40) stay in a file on the laptop so JARVIS remembers the context after a restart; the "New chat" button in its window empties it.
  • If the owner asks from their phone through their own private Telegram bot, the answer is delivered to them through Telegram.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server operated for JARVIS; there is none. JARVIS Cloud, if set up, lives in the owner's own Google account, as described above.

Other features (no Google data)

  • Recordings ("record this lecture"): the microphone and the laptop's sound are sent in chunks to Groq to be turned into text (or transcribed on the laptop when offline), and the transcript is sent to the AI model to write notes. The notes, transcript and audio are saved only on the laptop.
  • Camera: used only while the owner has turned on auto-lock, gestures, posture checks or the intruder alert, or when they say "look at this", "scan this" or ask where the laptop is. Camera frames are processed on the laptop and not saved, except as follows: a "look at this" photo is sent to the AI model to describe it; a scanned page is saved as a PDF on the laptop (in Documents\JARVIS Scans) and not sent anywhere; with the intruder alert on, if someone uses the locked laptop and doesn't unlock it within about 40 seconds, one webcam photo is sent to the owner's own Telegram chat; and when the owner asks "where's my laptop?", a webcam photo and a screenshot are sent to their Telegram chat.
  • Find my laptop: the location comes from Windows location services if they are on; otherwise ipinfo.io is asked for a rough location, which shares only the laptop's internet address with it. The result is sent only to the owner.
  • File search ("find the PDF about…"): an index of the text inside documents and photos of pages in the owner's Desktop, Documents, Downloads and Pictures folders is built and kept on the laptop (photos are read with Windows' own text recognition, on the laptop). When the owner searches, the names, folders and short matching extracts of the files found are sent to the AI model so it can answer.
  • Rewriting text ("fix the grammar", "make this formal"): the text the owner has selected is sent to the AI model, and the result is pasted back or left on the clipboard.
  • Expenses the owner tells JARVIS about are kept only in a file on the laptop. Tidying Downloads moves files between folders on the laptop and keeps a list of the moves so they can be undone; removed duplicates and installers go to the Recycle Bin.
  • Song recognition: a few seconds of audio are turned into a fingerprint on the laptop and sent to Shazam to identify the song.
  • Screen time is worked out on the laptop from the name of the app in use (never window titles or page contents) and kept on the laptop for 60 days. Clipboard history is kept in memory only, never written to disk or sent anywhere, and skips passwords and anything a password manager marks as private.
  • Pictures ("draw…"): the description is sent to Pollinations (or Google Gemini) to make the picture, which is saved on the laptop.
  • Answering for the owner (only while the owner has turned it on by voice): when someone sends the owner a direct WhatsApp message or SMS, its text and the chat's recent messages are sent to the AI model to write a short reply, which is sent from the owner's account. Group chats and automated senders (banks, one-time codes, shops) are never answered. If asked whether the reply is automatic, JARVIS says so honestly. When it answers a call, or places one the owner asked for, what the other person says is heard through the laptop's sound and sent to Groq to be turned into text, the conversation is sent to the AI model to decide what to say, and JARVIS's voice is made by Microsoft's online text-to-speech service. Callers are always told they are speaking to an assistant. Replies, call summaries and transcripts are sent only to the owner's own Telegram chat; nothing about these messages or calls is saved on disk, except the on/off setting and the list of people the owner asked JARVIS to leave alone.
  • Data analysis ("analyse my expenses sheet"): the column names, column types and first five rows of the file are sent to the AI model, which writes a calculation; the calculation runs on the laptop and its results are sent to the AI model to explain them. Charts are saved on the laptop.
  • Deep think and background research: the question, and for research the web pages read, are sent to the AI models (deep think asks up to three providers at the same time). Research reports are saved on the laptop in the JARVIS Notes folder.

Removing access

Saying "disconnect my Google account" revokes the token at Google and deletes it from the laptop. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and its date changed.
