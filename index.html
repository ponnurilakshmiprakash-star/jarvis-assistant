J.A.R.V.I.S.

A personal voice assistant that runs on its owner's Windows laptop.

What it is

JARVIS is a private, single-user assistant. It listens for its wake word, carries out spoken or typed requests on the laptop (opening apps, managing files, reminders, media, messages) and answers questions. It is not a public service and is not offered to other people.

Google sign-in

If its owner chooses, JARVIS can be connected to their own Google account with read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar, so it can answer requests such as "any new emails?" or "what's on my calendar tomorrow?" and give a reminder before events. It cannot send, delete, change or mark anything as read. The owner can disconnect it at any time.

Details are in the privacy policy.
